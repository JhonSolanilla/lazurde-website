Privacy Policy

LAZURDE RESORT · OMAN

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

Lazurde Resort is a private resort in the Sultanate of Oman. This policy explains how we handle the personal data you share with us when you use lazurdeom.com or make a booking. Contact: info@lazurdeom.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Your name, phone / WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Booking details: dates, number of guests, bed preferences, purpose of the stay and any discount code.
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Payments are processed by a payment provider licensed by the Central Bank of Oman. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic, anonymous website statistics. Your language choice may be stored on your own device.

3. Why we use it

  • To process, confirm and manage your booking, check-in, check-out and security deposit.
  • To contact you about your booking by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To meet our legal, tax and tourism obligations in Oman.
  • To improve our website and services. We only send you offers if you agree.

4. Who we share it with

Only with providers that help us run the service — the payment provider, our website host (Cloudflare) and messaging services (WhatsApp, email) — and with authorities when the law requires it. We never sell your data.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as needed to manage your booking and to meet legal and accounting requirements. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Under Oman's Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to info@lazurdeom.com.

7. Security

Our website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and we limit access to your data to the staff who need it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.

Según la Ley de Protección de Datos Personales de Omán (Real Decreto 6/2022), puedes pedir acceder, corregir o eliminar tus datos, o retirar tu consentimiento, escribiendo a info@lazurdeom.com.
